Fast & reliable support

General & Ordering

Everything you need to know about setting up an account and placing your first order with ElderBasket.

How do I place an order if I don't have a smartphone?

You don't need a smartphone or a computer to use ElderBasket! You can call our toll-free number at (800) 555-0199 anytime between 8 AM and 6 PM. One of our friendly customer service representatives will happily take your grocery list over the phone.

Is there a minimum order amount?

Yes, we maintain a very low minimum order amount of $20. We understand that sometimes you just need a few essential items like milk, bread, and medication, so we keep our minimums low to accommodate smaller weekly needs.

How do you handle out-of-stock items?

When an item is out of stock, our personal shoppers will proactively call you directly from the store. They will let you know what alternatives are available and ask for your approval before making any substitutions.

Can I set up a recurring weekly order?

Absolutely. With our ElderCare Premium plan, you can set up a "standing order" that automatically delivers your essential items on the exact same day every week. You can call or use the app to modify this list up to 24 hours before delivery.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express). In select participating zip codes, we also accept EBT/SNAP benefits for eligible food items. For safety reasons, our drivers do not carry or accept cash.

How can my children manage my account for me?

We offer a free Caregiver Dashboard. Adult children or caregivers can create a linked account to help build shopping lists, securely add payment methods, and receive real-time text message updates when deliveries arrive at your home.

Delivery & Safety

Information regarding delivery times, our "Kitchen Counter" assistance, and driver safety protocols.

Will the driver bring groceries inside my house?

Yes! Unlike standard delivery apps, ElderBasket offers an assisted 'Kitchen Counter' service. If you request it, our vetted drivers will bring the groceries inside, place them on your counter, and even help lift heavy items out of the bags.

Are your delivery drivers background-checked?

Absolutely. The safety of our community is our highest priority. Every single ElderBasket employee and driver undergoes a rigorous criminal background check, a driving record review, and specialized training for respectfully assisting seniors.

Do I need to be home to receive my delivery?

While we highly recommend being home to ensure perishables are stored quickly (and to utilize our inside-delivery assistance), you can opt for a "Contactless Porch Drop" if you prefer or if you have an appointment. We will send a photo confirmation once delivered.

How do you keep perishable items cold?

Food safety is critical. All refrigerated and frozen items are transported in premium, medical-grade insulated thermal bags packed with ice packs. Your perishables stay at safe, FDA-recommended temperatures from the store aisle to your kitchen.

What health and hygiene protocols do you follow?

Our drivers sanitize their hands before and after every delivery. They will gladly wear a mask upon request before entering your home. Additionally, delivery vehicles and thermal bags are rigorously disinfected daily.

Can I request the same driver every week?

Yes! ElderCare Premium members have the option to request a "Dedicated Shopper." This means the same familiar face will shop for you and deliver to you every week, building a trusting and friendly relationship.

Still have a question?

Our dedicated support team is available Monday - Friday, 8am to 6pm.
