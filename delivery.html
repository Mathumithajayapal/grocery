Our Process

What to Expect on Delivery Day

We've designed our delivery protocol to ensure you are comfortable, informed, and completely satisfied with every order.

1. Driver Assigned

You receive a text or automated phone call with your driver's name and photo when they leave the store.

2. Live ETA

Track your delivery in real-time. We'll alert you when we are approximately 5 minutes away from your home.

3. Arrival & Setup

We can leave items on the porch (contactless) or bring them directly to your kitchen counter based on your preference.

4. Quality Check

Before leaving, our driver confirms all items are present and intact. Satisfaction guaranteed.

Strict Protocols

Uncompromising Safety Standards

Because we serve a vulnerable community, our hygiene and safety standards go far beyond typical delivery apps.

Sanitization Rules

Drivers sanitize their hands before and after every delivery. All delivery vehicles and insulated bags are wiped down with medical-grade disinfectants daily.

Temperature Control

Dairy, meats, and frozen goods are transported in strictly monitored thermal bags to guarantee food safety and prevent spoilage during transit.

Vetted Personnel

Every Delivery Specialist undergoes a rigorous background check, driving record review, and specific training on interacting safely with senior citizens.
